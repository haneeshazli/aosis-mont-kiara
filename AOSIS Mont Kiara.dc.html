The story of the name
A Garden of

Air & Oasis

A name should carry a feeling before it carries a meaning. Ao speaks of air, light and openness; Oasis of shelter, water and calm — a green clearing held inside the city.

A secret haven awaits. Within colourful layers of metropolitan life. Enveloped by vibrance, embraced by nature. It is the joy of living with freedom, secluded from distraction.

Set within Mont Kiara's vibrant urban rhythm, AOSIS offers a private world made for retreat and connection. Here, everyday escape meets doorstep access to lifestyle highlights — lagoon water gardens, hammock groves and shaded timber decks layered through the podium, with living spaces lifted above the canopy.

The result is less a tower than a quiet address — a rare green treasure that asks you to slow down at the gate.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Q.

Who is the developer of AOSIS @ Mont Kiara?

AOSIS is developed by Modern Plus Sdn Bhd (200501010735 / 687782-V), a company wholly owned by JKG Land Berhad. Full developer licence and permit details are provided on request and stated in the official sales documents.

Q.

What unit types and sizes are available?

AOSIS comprises 236 residences plus 8 exclusive penthouses, sized from 1,648 sq ft up to 3,856 sq ft. Every home is a Semi-D in the sky with its own dedicated entrance lobby. Confirmed schedules are issued by the developer.

Q.

What does "Semi-D in the sky" mean?

Each floor is planned so that homes sit side by side like a semi-detached pair rather than along a shared corridor — giving dual-aspect frontage, cross ventilation and a private entrance lobby per residence.

Q.

Where exactly is AOSIS located?

On Jalan Kiara 5, Mont Kiara, Kuala Lumpur — a green-fronted site with access to the Penchala Link, SPRINT and DUKE highways, and minutes from the Mont Kiara retail and school belt.
